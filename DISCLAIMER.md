# Risk disclaimer

AHorizon is software for research, simulation, and supervised algorithmic trading. It is not investment, legal, accounting, or tax advice.

Trading stocks, ETFs, and cryptocurrency involves substantial risk. You may lose some or all of the funds committed to trading. Cryptocurrency markets can be particularly volatile and may have different regulatory and investor-protection arrangements from securities markets.

Historical results, backtests, simulations, and paper-trading results are hypothetical. They do not guarantee future performance and may not reflect actual liquidity, fees, spreads, slippage, latency, outages, taxes, or execution conditions.

Fee-aware filters use estimates. Actual trading costs may be higher, and a trade that appears profitable before execution may produce a loss after costs.

Users remain responsible for:

- Determining whether trading is appropriate for their circumstances.
- Understanding their broker's terms, fees, permissions, and risks.
- Protecting account credentials and authentication devices.
- Monitoring active automation and maintaining an emergency-stop procedure.
- Meeting applicable legal, regulatory, reporting, and tax obligations.

Availability of a feature does not mean that it is suitable or permitted for every user or jurisdiction.
