# Platform overview

AHorizon is a multi-user platform for testing and supervising algorithmic trading. Each customer creates an account, enables multi-factor authentication, and connects a broker account they control.

## Supported workflows

The portal provides areas for onboarding, portfolio information, performance, strategy explanations, activity history, settings, broker connections, and account security. Administrative functions support customer invitations and account management.

Paper trading is available before any real-money connection is activated. Paper results are simulations and should not be treated as evidence of future performance.

## Broker connections

AHorizon is designed to support:

- Interactive Brokers for stocks and ETFs.
- Kraken Pro for cryptocurrency.

Broker credentials should use the minimum permissions necessary for trading and account queries. Withdrawal permission must remain disabled. Deposits and withdrawals are performed directly through the broker.

## Controlled live testing

The initial live-testing workflow uses limited funding and conservative exposure controls. A customer must complete security setup, connection verification, risk acknowledgement, and a cooling period before live activation becomes available.

Live testing remains subject to market, broker, software, network, liquidity, and execution risk. A small pilot can reduce the amount at risk but cannot eliminate risk.

## Customer control

Customers retain control over their own brokerage accounts and may pause their AHorizon automation. Connecting an account and activating an account are separate actions.

## Intended audience

AHorizon is designed to make supervised algorithmic trading more understandable to customers who may not have professional trading experience. The interface emphasizes guided setup, plain-language explanations, visible risk limits, and clear system status.
